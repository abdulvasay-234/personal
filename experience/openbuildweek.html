Case study / Program & developer experience

OpenBuild Week

A connected journey from curiosity to building.

Interledger Foundation Local Impact Grant Awardee · 2026

OpenBuild Week is a flagship HackUnion program connecting campus activations, hands-on learning, mentors and building opportunities. My work spans program design, ecosystem coordination, developer experience, partnerships and execution.

My contribution

Program design · Campus activation · Developer experience · Partnerships

01 / The challenge

The starting point

A short event can create awareness without helping participants act. OpenBuild Week was designed with multiple touchpoints so learners could discover a technology, meet collaborators, get support and progress toward tangible work.

02 / The approach

How the experience connects

Campus touchpoints lead into learning, experimentation, mentorship and a final building experience.

03 / Work done

From intent to execution

Program architecture

Connected campus activations, hands-on workshops, expert talks, community sessions, mentorship and a hackathon / Builders Day into one participant journey.

Technical themes

Created learning opportunities around open payments, open source, AI and emerging technologies, with experimentation as the central activity.

Campus access

Program activity included Lords Skill Academy, Vardhaman College of Engineering and Lords Institute of Engineering & Technology.

Partner coordination

Worked across education, technology, community and opportunity partners, aligning access, tools and support around participants.

Grant-supported direction

The proposal was selected for Interledger Foundation Local Impact Grant support, connecting community learning with open payments and digital financial inclusion.

Design decisions

Used multiple entry points for different experience levels and connected workshops to mentors, community and later building opportunities to sustain momentum.

04 / Impact

What the work enabled

Multiple touchpoints

A structured program offered more than a single interaction with the ecosystem.

Practical progression

Sessions connected technical context to experimentation and building.

External support

Interledger Foundation grant selection supported the community-led program approach.

05 / Reflection

What I took forward

Developer experience depends on how content, people, tools and follow-up fit together. Each stage needs a clear purpose and a useful next step.
