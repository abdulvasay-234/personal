Case study / Developer ecosystem

HackUnion

An ecosystem that helps builders keep building.

HackUnion connects developers, designers, AI builders, founders and open-source contributors through practical learning, collaboration and opportunities to ship. My work spans ecosystem design, developer experience, programs, partnerships and execution.

01 / The challenge

The starting point

A session can create interest without creating a next step. HackUnion addresses the gap between consuming technical content and finding the people, feedback and support to build something with it.

02 / The approach

How the experience connects

A connected path from finding an opportunity to leaving with a project, contribution or next step.

03 / Work done

From intent to execution

Developer experience

Designed around preparation and registration before an experience, hands-on support during it, and feedback and continuity afterward.

Programs with a next step

Worked across OpenBuild Week and two GitHub Copilot Dev Days organized with Microsoft support, connecting technical learning to practical experimentation.

Community infrastructure

Connected organizers, community spaces, projects and showcases so participation could continue between events.

Partnerships with purpose

Coordinated relationships around access to tools, expertise, venues and opportunities that improve the participant experience.

04 / Impact

What the work enabled

Repeatable entry points

Programs and focused workshops give builders different ways to join and return.

Visible proof of work

Project-oriented experiences connect participation to making and sharing.

Shared infrastructure

Organizers, partners and community spaces support continuity beyond a single event.

05 / Reflection

What I took forward

The strongest community connections form when people build together. Projects, repeat participation and a clear next step tell a richer story than attendance alone.
